About Patel Brother Institute

Founded in Tharad with a simple aim: give local students consistent, well-structured academic support close to home.

Our story

Patel Brother Institute began operations on 1 August 2025 and has since been registered as a Micro Enterprise with the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (Udyam Registration Number UDYAM-GJ-04-0093196). The institute is based on Mitha Road, Tharad, in the Banas Kantha district of Gujarat.

What started as a small initiative to help neighbourhood students has grown into a dependable local institute, built on repeat trust rather than large-scale advertising.

What guides us

We believe learning works best in small groups, with clear expectations and steady follow-up — not one-off intensive sessions. Every batch is kept small enough that instructors know each student's progress by name.
